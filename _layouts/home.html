Current position

My current postdoctoral position has taken me to the Lagrange laboratory of the Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur (OCA) in Nice, France. There, I am working for the Interferometric Survey of Stellar Parameters (ISSP), funded by the European Research Council (ERC) under the European Union’s Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme (Grant agreement No. 101019653).
Among this project, I combine two main responsibilities. First, I have been recruited to characterise and optimise the SPICA-FT fringe-tracker operated by the CHARA interferometric array in mount Wilson observatory, California. Second, I lead the science program exploiting the SPICA interferometric beam combiner focused on the exoplanet host stars. Beyond these two aspects, I also contribute to the exploitation of SPICA through monthly remote observations performed from the plateau de Calern observatory, and supervise the internship of an undergraduate student. I also maintain collaborations with researchers from all around the world, including -but not limited to- IPAG (France), University of Köln (Germany), GSU (USA) and UNAM (Mexico).
My contract started on March, 2025 and ends on August, 2026. I am currently looking for my next position, and will be happy to discuss any possibility related to my main topics of interest, detailed in the dedicated sections.

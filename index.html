Hugo Nowacki, Ph.D.

Astrophysics postdoctoral researcher at the Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur (Nice, France)

I am currently a postdoc for the Interferometric Survey of Stellar Parameters (ISSP), led by Denis Mourard at the Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur (OCA, Nice, France).

My main focus is on characterising and optimising the SPICA-FT fringe tracker at the CHARA array.

My astrophysics research deals Young Stellar Objects (YSOs) and exoplanets.

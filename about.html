I am a 31 years old French, who loves spending time with his friends. But I am mostly a workoholic who has to put up his best effort to not spend too much time on his job, which happens to be one of my passions.

As a passionate young researcher, my curiosity is probably what defines me the best, along with my contemplative nature. I also have a sharp competitive spirit, and I enjoy a challenge more than a routine. I am a rather discrete person, as I was raised folowing the saying "speech is silver, but silence is golden".

I grew up in several places in France, but spent an important part of my teenages in Britanny where I lived 200 meters away from the sea. There, I developped my passion for free diving and spear fishing. This activity allows me to escape and enjoy the sea floors which I find particularly beautiful. It also developed my ability to remain calm in stressful situations. At the same time, I started playing handball, which I have played and coached for 15 years as it was a friendly environment in which to express my competitive nature. Later, near Grenoble, I discovered hiking in mountains was also a great way for me to escape and enjoy some time in nature. Nowadays, my activities are mostly centered around snorkeling, dancing latin dances (salsa, bachata) and playing music as an amateur (guitar and cajon).

After highschool, I joined preparatory classes for engineering school (french CPGE) and discovered my passion for Physics there. I enventually chose to go to the University Paris Saclay (Paris Sud at the time) to join the Bachelors of fundamental Physics I had a first Masters focused on astronomical and space-based engineering in Paris Saclay, my internship consisted in the design of a thermal control system for the JAXA/MMX space mission. Then, I moved to Grenoble and followed the Masters of astrophysics provided by Université Grenoble Alpes. My internship there was focused on the reconstruction of the magnetic field of a young star. I could not obtain a Ph.D. funding right after my internship, so I had a one year mission as a system engineer in charge to plan the activities of the Juventas cubesat onboard the ESA/Hera mission. I eventually did my Ph.D. in Grenoble, and worked on the observation of young Sun-like stars and on the GRAVITY+ instrument. Finally, I found my first postdoc in Nice, which is my current position.

Overall, I consider myself a quiet force, as I am capable of keeping my composure in very stressfull situations, while delivering to a high level of performance, whether it is on a sports field or in academic research.

I also contribute to the communication of scientific concepts to the grand public. I regularly participate in public observations of the night sky. Stargazing with strangers is always a great way to connect and to make them understand the importance of research in general, not just in astrophysics. I also realized two podcasts. One of them was hosted by Université Grenoble Alpes (UGA), as an episode of the "Allodocs" series. Each episode of this series aims at answering a naive question directly adressed by the science performed by the speaker, who is a Ph.D. candidate of UGA. The sixth episode of season 3 explains the difference between a star and a planet (in French) : I realized another podcast describing the work I performed during my Ph.D. more in details. This time, the episode was part of a short series aiming at describing the work of Ph.D. candidates at IPAG : "Podcastronomie" (still in French).

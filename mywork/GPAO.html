GRAVITY+

The GRAVITY+ project was officially launched in January, 2022. It represents a series of improvements of the GRAVITY instrument, but also of the whole VLTI facility at Paranal, Chile. There are three main components to GRAVITY+: first the GRAVIT-Wide module, which allows a much larger sky coverage by allowing the dual-field mode of GRAVITY to be performed with targets seperated by up to 60 arcseconds (rather 30 when considering anisoplanetism). This improvement is available, and is described in more details in This article

Theen, the Laser Guide Stars (LGS) allow for a correction of the tip-tilt induced by the atmosphere without the necessity a natural guide star nearby. This improvement increases the limiting G magnitude from 14 to 18 and contributes to the extension of the sky coverage, in combination with GRAVITY-Wide. It is available since 2026.

Finally, there is the GRAVITY+ Adaptive Optics system (GPAO) which is central to the whole project. By replacing the earlier MACAO system, the objective was to improve the Strehl ratio by a factor 2 for bright targets, and to enable the observation of much fainter targets overall, pushing the limiting K-band magnitude from 10 to 13. My contribution to GRAVITY+ is focused on this system, which will be described further.

The deformable mirrors and their mount

GPAO can be divided into several sub-parts. Each of these sub-part was the responsibility of a specific partner among the GRAVITY+ consortium. The Max Planck institute (MPE) in Graching, Germany was responsible for the wavefront sensor. The Meudon observatory in France (LESIA) was in charge of the real-time computeroperating the system. The Nice observatory (OCA) organised and hosted integration testings. Finally, the Grenoble observatory (IPAG) was in charge of the corrective Optics (CO). That is, the deformable mirors and the mounts that host them. This system itself can be decomposed in different sub-parts : the Deformable Mirrors (DM), the Quasi-Static Mount (QSM), the gimbal mount, the electronic rack and the cooling system.
